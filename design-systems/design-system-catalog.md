# Design System Catalog

Use this catalog before loading a full Design MD. A system is available for direct use only when its status is `validated`.

| System ID | Display name | Purpose | Audience | Content form | Output medium | Status | Design MD | Asset |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `editorial-longform` | Editorial Longform | Learning, deep reading, reference and thoughtful sharing | Self, friends, colleagues and public readers | Course notes, video or podcast summaries, deep articles, methods and knowledge documents | Standalone HTML webpage; printable from browser | `validated` | `editorial-longform-design.md` | `../assets/editorial-longform-template.html` |
| `editorial-bilingual-longform` | Editorial Bilingual Longform | Faithful bilingual reading and source comparison | Readers who primarily read Chinese and sometimes verify the English source, plus English-first readers | Finished English articles, including articles faithfully reconstructed upstream from audio or video | Interactive standalone HTML webpage; printable bilingual document | `validated` | `editorial-bilingual-longform-design.md` | `../assets/editorial-bilingual-longform-template.html` |

## Selection note

- Recommend `Editorial Longform` for a finished monolingual long-form document with a continuous editorial reading path.
- Recommend `Editorial Bilingual Longform` when the source is primarily English and the final article must support Chinese reading, original-English verification, and an uninterrupted bilingual mode.
- Neither system is intended for slide presentations, dashboards, transactional interfaces, campaign landing pages, sales-led proposals, or synchronized media players.
