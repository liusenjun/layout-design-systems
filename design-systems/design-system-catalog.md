# Design System Catalog

Use this catalog before loading a full Design MD. A system is available for direct use only when its status is `validated`.

| System ID | Display name | Purpose | Audience | Content form | Output medium | Status | Design MD | Asset |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `editorial-longform` | Editorial Longform | Learning, deep reading, reference and thoughtful sharing | Self, friends, colleagues and public readers | Course notes, video or podcast summaries, deep articles, methods and knowledge documents | Standalone HTML webpage; printable from browser | `validated` | `editorial-longform-design.md` | `../assets/editorial-longform-template.html` |

## Selection note

`Editorial Longform` is the only registered system in version 1.0. Recommend it when the user needs a long, continuously scrollable reading document with clear hierarchy and editorial rhythm. Do not recommend it for slide presentations, dashboards, transactional interfaces, campaign landing pages, or sales-led proposals.
